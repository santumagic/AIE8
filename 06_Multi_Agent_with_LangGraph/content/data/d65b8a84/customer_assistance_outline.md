# Customer Assistance Outline

## Common Use-Cases

1. **Product Inquiries**:  
   Customers frequently ask about product features, specifications, and availability.

2. **Order Status**:  
   Users often request updates regarding the status of their orders, including shipping details and estimated delivery times.

3. **Returns and Exchanges**:  
   Handling queries related to the return process, exchange policies, and refund statuses is common.

4. **Technical Support**:  
   Customers may need assistance with troubleshooting, installation, or maintenance of products.

5. **Account Management**:  
   Issues related to account creation, password resets, and profile updates are frequent.

6. **Billing and Payments**:  
   Queries about billing cycles, payment methods, invoice requests, and subscription management.

7. **Feedback and Complaints**:  
   Customers often provide feedback on products/services, and they raise complaints which need to be addressed.

8. **Promotions and Discounts**:  
   Inquiries about current promotions, discount codes, and eligibility for special offers are common.

## Common Domains

1. **E-commerce**:  
   Online retail businesses often encounter inquiries related to product sales, customer accounts, and order processing.

2. **Telecommunications**:  
   Customers frequently ask about service plans, data usage, and billing issues.

3. **Technology**:  
   Companies that produce software and hardware regularly receive support requests and technical inquiries.

4. **Travel and Hospitality**:  
   Common inquiries relate to booking, cancellations, customer service, and loyalty programs in this domain.

5. **Banking and Finance**:  
   Customers often inquire about account management, transactions, loans, and investment services.
