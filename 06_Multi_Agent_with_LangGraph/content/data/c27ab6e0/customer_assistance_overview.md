# Customer Assistance Overview

## Common Use-Cases

1. **Customer Support**: Assisting customers with inquiries related to products, services, or policies.
2. **Technical Support**: Helping users troubleshoot issues with devices, software, or applications.
3. **Order Management**: Providing information on order status, changes, cancellations, and refunds.
4. **Account Assistance**: Assisting users with account creation, password resets, and profile updates.
5. **Product Information**: Offering detailed descriptions, specifications, and use-case scenarios for products.
6. **Billing Inquiries**: Addressing questions related to billing, payments, invoices, and subscriptions.
7. **Feedback Collection**: Gathering user feedback on services or products for improvement.
8. **FAQs**: Responding to frequently asked questions to provide quick answers.

## Common Domains

1. **E-commerce**: Platforms selling products online, handling orders, returns, and customer inquiries.
2. **Technology**: Software and hardware companies providing technical support for their products.
3. **Finance**: Banks and financial institutions assisting customers with accounts, loans, and transactions.
4. **Telecommunications**: Service providers helping with network issues, billing, and account management.
5. **Travel and Hospitality**: Airlines and hotels managing bookings, changes, and customer service questions.
6. **Healthcare**: Medical services addressing patient inquiries, appointments, and billing.
7. **Entertainment**: Streaming services and content providers assisting users with subscriptions and content access.
